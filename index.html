Our story

A Taste of Punjab in Bengaluru

Traditional Punjabi cooking, made for the everyday rhythm of Electronic City.

Punjab Ki Rasoi opened in 2021 on Velankani Road, a short walk from the tech parks of Electronic City Phase I. The idea was simple: honest Punjabi food, cooked the way it is cooked at home and in the dhabas of the North — slow-simmered dals, gravies built on fresh onion and tomato, and breads pulled from a live tandoor.

The menu is built for everyday eating rather than occasion dining. A paratha plate or thali on a workday lunch, butter chicken and garlic naan to share in the evening, a biryani packed to take home. Portions are generous, the tandoor runs all day, and there is a chair for everyone — families, colleagues and solo diners alike.

  • Fresh ingredients, gravies made in small batches through the day
  • Live tandoor for rotis, naan, kulcha and kebabs
  • Veg and non-veg kitchen · dine-in, takeaway and delivery
  • Family-friendly, open every day 10 AM – 11:30 PM
