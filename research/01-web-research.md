# Restaurant Website Research — 2026

## Hard numbers
- 70%+ of restaurant site traffic is mobile. Design mobile-first, not "responsive later".
- Page load over 3s = 53% abandon. Each extra second = +7% order abandonment.
- Sub-3s sites convert 2.5–3x better.
- Strategic CTA placement = +28% conversions.
- 76% of diners prefer ordering direct over third-party apps (aggregator commission is 18–30% in India).

## Must-have elements (consensus across sources)
1. Mobile-optimised homepage with one obvious CTA
2. **HTML menu, never a PDF** — PDFs do not rank, do not read well on phone, cannot be updated fast
3. Real food photography on the menu itself
4. Online ordering or reservation path
5. Location + hours visible without scrolling on mobile
6. Click-to-call phone number
7. Embedded Google Maps

## Local SEO
- NAP identical everywhere (site, Google Business Profile, Zomato, Swiggy, JustDial).
- `Restaurant` + `Menu` schema.org JSON-LD markup — tells Google what you serve and where.
- Google Business Profile is effectively the real homepage. Keep hours/photos/menu link current.

## Design references
- **Dishoom** — Awwwards winner. Per-location founding story, strong narrative.
- **Dhamaka** — bold red palette, dishes shown immediately, menus split brunch/dinner/drinks/dessert.
- 2026 trend: multi-platform ordering/reservation routing inside one site.

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- Low-quality/stock-looking imagery
- Stale hours
- PDF menus
- Hero video autoplay that blocks load
